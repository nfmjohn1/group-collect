🎉 Welcome to Group Collect!

Your automated Facebook group management assistant

1

Navigate to Your Facebook Group

Open your Facebook group's member requests page. Click the extension icon to open the popup.

2

Configure Google Sheets (Optional)

Go to Settings and add your Google Sheets URL. This allows you to automatically export member data to your spreadsheet.

3

Collect Member Data

Click "Collect Members" to scrape pending member requests. All data is saved locally and can be exported anytime.

4

Set Up Auto-Decline Rules (Optional)

Configure automatic decline rules based on email requirements or unanswered questions to save time reviewing requests.
